01 — About us

More than an engineering firm: an innovation consultancy.

EMMA was founded in 2012 at COPPE/UFRJ, the engineering research institute of the Federal University of Rio de Janeiro, and has spent more than ten years in the oil and gas sector as the engineering arm of HiCad Brasil.

We bridge the gap between large companies that need innovative solutions and small businesses and start-ups looking for a way to present their ideas. We believe no challenge is impossible and that every problem has more than one solution.

  • A team and partner network covering every engineering discipline
  • Technology development with COPPE/UFRJ laboratories
  • End-to-end service, without the cost of a middleman

02 — Services

What we do

Three complementary areas: measuring with precision, digitising what already exists and designing the right solution for each client.

Dimensional Control

Precise measurement of parts, sub-assemblies and complete equipment. Less rework, fewer last-minute adjustments, lower labour costs and higher-quality assembly.

  • Assembly dimensional control
  • Fabrication control
  • Clash analysis
  • Topographic surveying

As-Built & Digital Twin

3D laser scanning and modelling of plants, platforms and terminals. Accurate documentation of what was actually built, and a digital replica ready for design, maintenance and operations.

  • 3D laser scanning
  • Point clouds and modelling
  • As-Built documentation
  • Digital Twin

Consultancy & Digital Transformation

Tailored solutions that adapt the most innovative technologies on the market to each client's reality. From a simple measurement project to full IIoT integration.

  • IIoT integration
  • New technologies with COPPE/UFRJ
  • Equipment adaptation
  • Technical partnerships
